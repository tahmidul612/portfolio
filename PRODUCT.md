# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers and engineering leads, often on a phone, who arrive from a resume, LinkedIn or a GitHub profile. In about thirty seconds they want to know who Tahmidul is, what they work on now, and whether their best projects are real. The resume and contact details are one tap away. Developers who come from a README are a secondary audience and read the project pages in depth.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for an automation engineer. Success means a reader leaves knowing their current role, has seen one or two projects that show real work (demos and explainer videos), and can reach them or download the resume without hunting.

## Brand Personality

Calm and curious, technical without showing off. "Deep-sea glow": near-black blue water with cyan light that responds to the visitor, like bioluminescence; in light mode, sunlit shallows. Modern and a little futuristic, but minimal and elegant. Copy is plain, first person and a bit self-deprecating ("my laziness compels me…"), written the way they'd explain a project to a friend.

## Anti-references

- Generic developer-portfolio templates: identical card grids, skill bars, "Hi, I'm X 👋" heroes.
- Neon cyberpunk, terminal cosplay, monospace everywhere.
- Glassmorphism as decoration, gradient text, hero-metric blocks.
- The old Congo/Hugo site: cramped mobile nav, ragged cards, wrapping badges.

## Design Principles

1. Phone first. Every page is designed at 390 px before desktop.
2. Show the work. Demos and videos beat adjectives.
3. Light with purpose. Glow marks what you can interact with and what has focus.
4. Fast by default. Zero JS unless a feature needs it; images optimized at build.
5. Honest copy: no inflated claims; status badges say when something is unfinished.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both themes. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (no drift, no pointer lamp, no view transitions). All interactive targets at least 44 px on touch. Captions on every video.
